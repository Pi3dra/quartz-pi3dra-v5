#WIP 

This project is actually part of a university course of **Network Programming**

The idea is to program the different components of a re-configured [adeept rasptank](https://www.adeept.com/adeept-rasptank-wifi-wireless-smart-robot-car-kit-for-raspberry-pi-3-model-b-b-2b-tank-tracked-robot-with-4-dof-robotic-arm-opencv-target-tracking_p0121_s0032.html).
To play a capture the flag game with many other tanks, and a game master server.

The flag will be a white paper on the ground, which can be detected with sensors below the tank.
there will be an IR sensor and an IR emitter to shoot other tanks and make them "drop" their flag.
then to succesfully deposit a flagwe have to scan a QR Code that's in our base, and notify the game master server properly.

This in currently on the works, and I'll write more on it later.
